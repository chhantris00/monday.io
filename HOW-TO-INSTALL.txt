MONDAY - how to put it on your phone
====================================

Monday is a web app. To install it on Android or iPhone, it must be hosted at a
web address (https). Hosting is free. Your API key is NOT inside these files; you
type it in on each device and it stays on that device.

STEP 1 - Put the files online (pick one, about 2 minutes)
  A) Netlify Drop (easiest): on a computer, open app.netlify.com/drop, then drag
     this whole unzipped "monday-app" folder onto the page. You get an address
     like https://something.netlify.app
  B) Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages: upload the folder as a new site.

STEP 2 - Open the address on your phone and install
  Android (Chrome): open the address, tap the menu (three dots), then
     "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  iPhone (Safari): open the address in Safari, tap the Share icon, then
     "Add to Home Screen".

STEP 3 - Connect it (free option)
  1. Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Choose "Get API key", create a key and copy it.
  3. Open Monday, leave "Google Gemini (free tier)" selected, paste the key,
     press Connect, then tap the big button and talk.
  The free tier has daily and per-minute limits. Google may use free-tier
  prompts to improve its products, so keep private material for a paid plan.
  If you have an Anthropic key instead, pick "Anthropic Claude" in the same box.
  Settings also has "Other (OpenAI-compatible)" for providers such as Groq or
  OpenRouter.

VOICE NOTES
  Android Chrome: voice input works, also in the installed app.
  iPhone: voice input works in Safari. In the installed Home Screen app, Apple
     blocks it, so use the microphone key on the iPhone keyboard to dictate.
     Monday speaks its answers on both.

PRIVACY
  Anyone who knows your web address can open the page, but it is empty without
  their own API key. Do not paste your key on a device you do not own.
  Your key is not inside these files.
